## Slide 1: Problem Statement

**Telecom companies lose millions to customer churn**

- Acquiring new customers costs 5x more than retaining existing ones
- Need to identify: Who will churn? Why? How to prevent it?

---

## Slide 2: Dataset Overview

- **7,043 customers** | **22 features** | **26.5% churn rate**
- Demographics, services, account info, churn status

---

## Slide 3: Key Churn Drivers

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Month-to-month contract | 42.7% churn |
| 0-1 year tenure | 47.7% churn |
| Fiber optic service | Highest churn segment |

---

## Slide 4: Predictive Models

| Model | Accuracy | Best For |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Logistic Regression | 77.3% | Interpretability |
| Random Forest | 75.4% | Feature importance |

**Optimal threshold: 0.180** — catches 77% of churners

---

## Slide 5: Business Recommendations

1. **Loyalty discounts** for month-to-month customers
2. **Better onboarding** for first-year customers
3. **Fiber optic pricing review** — bundled offers
4. **Early warning system** using predictive model

---

## Slide 6: Expected Impact

- **15-20% churn reduction** in first year
- **Rs. 93,750 net benefit** per 1,000 customers
- **ROI:** Model pays for itself in 3 months
